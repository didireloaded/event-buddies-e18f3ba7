# **Event Buddies 2.0**

100 feature ideas to make it more useful, social, interactive and genuinely fun at festivals.

I think Event Buddies has the potential to become much more than a festival map and a way to find friends.

Imagine someone using the app before Sound of Summer to plan their day, during the festival to connect with friends, discover DJs, play interactive games and participate in challenges, then after the event to create a personalised festival recap.

**That's the direction I would take. A practical festival companion with a fun social experience built around real events.**

I also looked at current festival apps such as Frontstage and FestMates. Personal schedules, offline maps, friend coordination and reminders are increasingly established features. Attendee feedback also shows how frustrating it can be when basic lineups and maps stop working without internet.&#x20;

# **The complete feature library**

Some of these improve functions you already have. Others are new ideas. I'm deliberately including both so we can decide what Event Buddies should eventually become.

## **1. Core festival features**

01–10 · The essentials everyone needs

1. **Instant Event Explorer.** Browse an event without having to register or complete onboarding.
2. **Personal Festival Dashboard.** Your event, saved artists, crew plans and important shortcuts.
3. **My Ticket.** Save your ticket locally and open it instantly at the entrance.
4. **Offline Festival Pack.** Save the venue map, lineup, rules and key information before arriving.
5. **Unified Search.** Search for artists, venues, facilities, activities and event information in one place.
6. **Event Timeline.** A simple before, during and after event experience.
7. **Official Announcements.** Verified schedule changes, entrance information and event updates.
8. **My Festival Plan.** Combine favourite artists, meeting times and personal reminders.
9. **Quick Action Bar.** Immediate access to Map, Ticket, Crew and Safety.
10. **Multi-Event Support.** Eventually browse and save multiple verified festivals, not only Sound of Summer.

## **2. DJ, music and lineup features**

11–20 · Music discovery and planning

11. **Live DJ Schedule.** View confirmed performances by stage and time.
12. **Now Playing / Up Next.** Show the current and upcoming artist based on the latest official timetable.
13. **Favourite DJs.** Save artists and build a personalised lineup.
14. **Performance Reminders.** Optional reminders before saved sets, with device-dependent delivery.
15. **Set Clash Detector.** Warn when two favourite artists perform at overlapping times.
16. **Artist Profiles.** Photos, biographies, music genres and verified social links.
17. **Music Preview.** Open selected songs or official playlists through licensed music services.
18. **Find My DJ on the Map.** Jump from an artist's profile to their confirmed stage.
19. **Shared Crew Lineup.** See which artists your friends have saved and where your schedules overlap.
20. **Discover My Next DJ.** Suggest artists based on genres and existing favourites.

## **3. Interactive venue map features**

21–30 · Finding everything at the festival

21. **Smart Map Filters.** Quickly display stages, bars, food, toilets, gates and medical points.
22. **Find Facilities.** A dedicated screen for nearby facilities relative to a selected landmark.
23. **Personal Map Pins.** Save the location of a parked car, meeting point or favourite vendor.
24. **Crew Meeting Pins.** Display a shared meeting landmark directly on the venue plan.
25. **Stage Information.** Tap a stage to see its confirmed lineup and schedule.
26. **Venue Walking Routes.** Show routes using verified internal pathways, once reliable map data exists.
27. **Accessible Venue Routes.** Highlight wheelchair-accessible paths, facilities and viewing areas.
28. **Festival Zone Explorer.** Explain VIP, Experience, Platinum and other verified access zones.
29. **Map Updates.** Let organisers revise pins and distribute clearly versioned map updates.
30. **Offline Saved Locations.** Keep personal landmarks and the essential venue information accessible without mobile data.

## **4. Friends, crew and social features**

31–40 · Keeping everyone together

31. **Private Crews.** Create a friend group with a name and invitation code.
32. **QR Friend Connect.** Add a friend face-to-face by scanning their code.
33. **Crew Meeting Planner.** Pick a place, agree on a time and share the plan with everyone.
34. **Live Friend Location.** Optional, time-limited GPS sharing between consenting crew members when connected.
35. **Quick Status Updates.** "At the bar", "Watching Main Stage", "Getting food" or custom status messages.
36. **Crew Chat.** Private text messaging with a connected backend and offline drafts.
37. **Regroup Now.** Create and distribute a new meeting plan when the group gets separated.
38. **Crew Polls.** Vote on which stage to visit, where to eat or when to leave.
39. **Shared Travel Plans.** Coordinate drivers, shuttle information and agreed pickup locations.
40. **Safe Check-In.** Send a simple "I'm okay" confirmation to selected friends, without automatically exposing GPS.

## **5. Fun party features**

41–50 · Things people would actually use at a party

41. **Party Screen.** Turn the phone display into a bright solid colour selected by the user.
42. **Big Text Sign.** Display messages such as "I'm here", "Meet at the gate" or your crew name in enormous text.
43. **Crew Colour Sync.** Everyone in a group chooses the same screen colour to help recognise one another.
44. **Party Countdown.** A large shared countdown to a headliner or official festival moment.
45. **Crowd Mood Poll.** Vote on the current atmosphere using simple, fun choices.
46. **Song of the Night.** Attendees nominate and vote for favourite tracks.
47. **Festival Shoutouts.** Submit short messages for moderated organiser approval.
48. **Dance Challenge.** Join an official challenge, record a clip and optionally share it.
49. **Crew Photo Timer.** A distraction-free countdown for taking group photographs.
50. **Spin the Next Stage.** A playful stage picker for people who don't know where to go next.

These features would live in a dedicated Party area rather than cluttering the main navigation.

## **6. Games, challenges and festival competitions**

51–60 · Make attending the festival more playful

51. **Festival Scavenger Hunt.** Find approved checkpoints around the venue and collect digital stamps.
52. **Festival Bingo.** Complete challenges like visiting all three stages or discovering a new DJ.
53. **DJ Trivia.** Answer questions about artists performing at the festival.
54. **Crew Challenges.** Complete small group missions with your friends.
55. **Stage Explorer.** Collect a digital stamp after visiting each stage.
56. **Festival Predictions.** Predict favourite performances, surprise moments or audience poll results without betting.
57. **Challenge Wheel.** Spin for a safe, optional task such as taking a creative group photo.
58. **Best Festival Outfit.** Submit an outfit photo for a moderated community showcase.
59. **Sponsor Treasure Trail.** Visit participating sponsor activations and collect stamps through official QR codes.
60. **Hidden Achievements.** Unlock badges for completing certain activities throughout the festival.

For Sound of Summer, I'd build the first challenge around the three stages. That gives people an actual reason to explore the venue instead of standing in one area all day.

Any physical challenges should use approved areas and avoid encouraging unsafe behaviour or interfering with performers.

## **7. Festival camera, content and memories**

61–70 · Create content people want to share

61. **Festival Camera.** Take photos with official event frames and overlays.
62. **Personal Photo Diary.** Keep a private collection of photos taken during the event.
63. **Moment Bookmarks.** Save a stage, timestamp and short note when something memorable happens.
64. **Crew Photo Album.** Create a private shared gallery for friends.
65. **Photo Missions.** Prompts such as "Capture your favourite stage moment."
66. **Official Aftermovie Submissions.** Attendees can submit selected clips with clear consent and usage-rights approval.
67. **Personal Festival Recap.** Create a shareable visual summary of the experience.
68. **My Lineup Poster.** Automatically design a story-format poster featuring your favourite artists.
69. **Crew Yearbook.** Generate a group memory page containing shared photographs and highlights.
70. **Festival Memory Archive.** Keep a record of attended events, collected badges and personal recaps across different editions.

I'd particularly develop the Aftermovie Submissions idea. Imagine getting authentic crowd footage from different parts of Sound of Summer. With proper permissions and moderation, that could become useful for the event's official aftermovie.

## **8. Food, vendors, transport and convenience**

71–80 · The practical side of a festival

71. **Vendor Directory.** Browse verified food vendors, merchandise stands and available services.
72. **Food Menus.** See menus and prices provided by participating vendors.
73. **Accepted Payments.** Find out whether stalls accept cash, cards or an approved cashless system.
74. **Queue Information.** Show verified vendor updates about waiting times when that data is available.
75. **Water Stations.** Locate confirmed drinking-water facilities.
76. **Charging Points.** Find verified phone-charging services or power stations.
77. **Shuttle Information.** View official departure times, pickup points and route information.
78. **Find My Ride.** Save parking details, a photograph and a pickup landmark.
79. **Lost and Found.** Submit an item description securely and contact the official lost-property team.
80. **Accessibility Guide.** View wheelchair facilities, accessible entrances, support areas and assistance contacts.

## **9. Safety, privacy and reliability**

81–90 · Features people can depend on

81. **Emergency Map.** One-tap access to medical points, security and verified exits.
82. **Emergency Contacts.** Direct call actions for official, verified emergency numbers.
83. **Verified Information Labels.** Distinguish confirmed information from pending details.
84. **Low-Battery Mode.** Reduce unnecessary animations, background activity and refresh requests.
85. **Expiring Location Sharing.** Automatically stop live sharing after a chosen time.
86. **Granular Sharing Permissions.** Choose whether friends can see a meeting point, status or live position.
87. **Block and Report.** Controls for unwanted contact if public social features are introduced.
88. **Outdoor Visibility Mode.** High-contrast layouts and larger controls for daylight conditions.
89. **Language Options.** Start with English and consider Afrikaans, Oshiwambo and other relevant languages with properly reviewed translations.
90. **Offline Status Indicator.** Clearly show whether the saved event content is available and which connected features are unavailable.

I would keep the safety and privacy features separate from party games. Those controls must always be predictable and accessible.

## **10. Organiser, sponsor and future platform features**

91–100 · Turn Event Buddies into something organisers can use

91. **Organiser Dashboard.** Manage event details, stages, artists and venue facilities.
92. **Official Event Updates.** Publish schedule changes, safety information and operational notices.
93. **Lineup Management.** Update performance times, cancellations and stage assignments.
94. **Vendor Management.** Give authorised vendors controlled access to update their listings.
95. **Venue Issue Reporting.** Let attendees report problems with a location or facility to the appropriate team.
96. **Sponsor Campaigns.** Offer approved interactive activations, branded challenges and rewards.
97. **Live Stage Polls.** Run official audience interactions with results that can be displayed on festival screens.
98. **Event Analytics.** Show organisers privacy-conscious aggregated engagement and feature-usage metrics.
99. **Multi-Festival Platform.** Use reusable event templates and isolated organiser accounts to support different festivals.
100. **Official Ticketing Integration.** Connect to Howler or another ticketing provider only through an authorised integration.

## **How I would organise the interface**

I wouldn't put every feature into the bottom navigation. That would make the app overwhelming.

Instead, I'd keep four primary sections and let the fun features appear naturally within them.

Home

Lineup

Map

Crew

Supporting experiences

**Party**

Games, challenges, Party Screen and Festival Quest

**Memories**

Festival Camera, shared photos, passport and recap

Party and Memories can be reached through Home and contextual entry points. Ticket, Safety and Profile remain easy to access without requiring extra permanent tabs.

## **Three different directions Event Buddies could take**

**Direction A. The essential festival companion**

Best at maps, lineups, tickets, meetups and offline reliability. Smaller scope, easier to launch and maintain.

**Direction B. The social festival companion**

Keeps those essentials but adds private crews, group plans, Party Screen, Festival Passport, creative challenges and shareable memories. This is the direction I'd choose.

**Direction C. The complete festival platform**

Adds an organiser dashboard, real-time services, official ticketing connections, vendor tools, sponsored experiences and multiple festivals. More ambitious, but significantly more complex.

One idea I particularly like is **Festival Passport + Party Screen + Crew Mode** as the three signature experiences.

They complement each other rather than competing. Crew Mode helps people stay together, Party Screen gives them something playful to use in the moment, and Festival Passport encourages them to explore and gives them something to remember afterwards.

And importantly, much of that can be built without turning the application into a heavy social network or requiring constant connectivity.

**My choice would be Direction B, built on the reliability of Direction A.** Start with Sound of Summer as the first real event, learn which features attendees actually use, and only then expand toward the full festival platform.
